Commissioning services

A scope matched to your building and systems.

01

Whole-building commissioning

Verification of building systems for new construction and major renovations. Our healthcare experience includes urgent care, mental health and primary care facilities, with construction and warranty-phase commissioning work.

02

HVAC & electrical systems

Functional testing of air handlers, chillers and controls, alongside electrical infrastructure such as transformers, switchgear, automatic transfer switches and distribution panels. The scope follows the systems and requirements of each project.

03

Retrocommissioning

Investigation and testing of existing systems to identify operational deficiencies and improvement measures. Work can include site-specific plans, field inspections, equipment inventories, operating-data analysis and recommendations with cost estimates.

04

Central plants & CHP

Commissioning of central chiller plants and combined heat and power systems, including the interaction of generators, thermal systems and controls. Newington VAMC demonstrates how functional testing can reveal a problem in an integrated plant's control sequence.

05

Solar PV commissioning

Commissioning services for renewable-energy installations. Alares' project experience includes VA solar PV systems in Houston, Tampa and Bay Pines.

Process & deliverables

Test the systems. Document the findings.

01

Plan the verification

Define the commissioning scope around the facility, systems and project requirements. Develop test procedures or a site-specific retrocommissioning plan, with field inspections and equipment inventories appropriate to the assignment.

02

Test & track issues

Review test-and-balance information, perform prefunctional and functional performance testing, and document deficiencies in an issues log. Work with the project team on corrective actions and verify system performance against the project requirements.

03

Support operations

Deliver testing records and systems documentation for the owner's operations team. Depending on the assignment, deliverables include a systems manual, training verification, warranty-phase services or retrocommissioning recommendations with cost estimates and projected savings.
