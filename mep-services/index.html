MEP services

Engineering the systems your facility depends on.

01

Mechanical & central plants

Design for heating, cooling and utility infrastructure, from individual air handlers to hospital boiler plants and campus distribution systems.

  • HVAC design, air handler and chiller replacements
  • Boiler plants and central utility plant upgrades
  • Steam, condensate and chilled-water piping
  • Load calculations, energy modeling and system performance analysis
02

Electrical engineering

Electrical design coordinated with building loads, mechanical equipment and the facility's existing distribution infrastructure.

  • Power distribution, transformers and switchgear upgrades
  • Emergency and backup power systems
  • Interior and exterior lighting and controls
  • Electrical load analysis, solar PV and combined heat and power integration
03

Plumbing engineering

Water, drainage and healthcare utility systems for renovations, equipment replacements and new facilities.

  • Sanitary and storm drainage
  • Medical gas systems for healthcare facilities
  • Water conservation and plumbing system upgrades
Design process & deliverables

From existing conditions to construction documents.

01

Investigate & define

Review the facility's systems and project requirements with the owner's team. Existing-condition surveys, equipment assessments and load calculations inform the design scope, preliminary concepts and layouts.

02

Model & coordinate

Develop the engineering design and coordinate equipment, piping and utilities with the other project disciplines. On complex retrofits, 3D scanning and Revit modeling help document the spaces where new systems must fit.

03

Document the work

Prepare construction drawings, specifications and cost estimates. Our Albany and Manchester projects show how field investigation and coordinated models carry through into documents for bidding and construction.
