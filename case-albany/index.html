Hospital boiler plant design at Albany VA Medical Center.

Alares served as prime designer for a central utility plant upgrade at the Albany VA Medical Center: replacing aging boilers, coordinating steam and condensate piping, and designing electrical and backup power upgrades for an operating hospital campus.

The hospital infrastructure challenge

The Albany campus depends on steam for sterilization, kitchen and laundry operations, humidification and building heat. Replacing the plant's aging boilers required an understanding of the existing equipment and distribution network, along with the VA's requirements for the facility.

The design scope extended beyond the boilers to the central utility plant's switchgear, transformers and a new backup generator. Alares coordinated these systems as part of its healthcare MEP and central utility plant engineering work.

Alares' role and design scope

  • Prime design coordination. Led the boiler replacement design and coordinated with the Albany VA engineering team.
  • Existing conditions. Investigated boiler plant equipment and the campus steam and condensate piping.
  • Mechanical design. Developed replacement boiler and piping designs, supported by engineering calculations.
  • Electrical infrastructure. Coordinated upgrades to 13.2 kV switchgear, associated 480V transformers and backup generation for the boiler plant.
  • Multidiscipline coordination. Integrated the mechanical, structural, electrical, civil and fire protection work needed for the plant upgrade.

Documenting the plant before design

Alares developed a 3D Revit model of the existing boiler plant and piping. The model gave the design team a common view of equipment arrangements and pipe routes while preparing the replacement design.

Investigation also identified a chimney stack issue affecting air quality requirements. Alares addressed it with the VA engineering team during design. This existing-conditions work helped define the scope before construction.

Project deliverables

  • Preliminary concepts, layouts and program development
  • Existing-conditions investigations and a 3D Revit model
  • Coordinated contract drawings and specifications
  • Piping calculations and construction cost estimates
