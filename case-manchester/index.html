Case study / 05

Hospital steam system replacement design at Manchester VA.

Alares designed steam and condensate piping replacement at the Manchester VA Medical Center. Remote tunnel surveys and 3D scanning documented aging infrastructure beneath active offices and patient care areas before the team prepared the construction documents.

The challenge: surveying an operating hospital

The existing steam distribution system had reached the end of its useful life. Replacement covered the main steam and condensate piping from the boiler plant to Building 1, plus steam distribution inside the main building.

A 500-foot subbasement tunnel ran below occupied offices and patient care areas. Parts were only four feet high, and the tunnel was classified as a confined space. The mechanical room presented a different challenge: a dense arrangement of pipes and valves that needed to be documented for design.

Alares' engineering scope

  • Design of replacement steam and condensate piping between the boiler plant and Building 1
  • Design of replacement steam distribution piping throughout the main building
  • Electrical and fire protection engineering
  • Coordination with civil engineering and asbestos testing
  • Construction drawings, specifications and cost estimates

The project illustrates the existing-conditions investigation behind our hospital MEP and steam system engineering services.

Remote survey and 3D documentation

Alares used a remote-access vehicle fitted with three cameras to document the tunnel. Video captured steam traps, valves and other components without requiring surveyors to enter the confined space for that survey. The footage was also provided to bidding contractors as a record of the existing conditions.

In the main mechanical room, the team used 3D scanning to build a Revit BIM model. On-site surveys confirmed and labeled pipes and valves. The model was incorporated into the design documents to communicate the routing and arrangement of the replacement systems.

What the owner and bidders received

  • Documented tunnel conditions. Video of the piping route and components for design and bidding.
  • A coordinated mechanical room model. 3D Revit documentation supported by field surveys.
  • A construction design package. Drawings, specifications and cost estimates for the steam and condensate replacement.
