The portfolio challenge

The VA wanted to find ways to reduce energy costs across its New England hospital network through the operation of existing equipment. Each campus had a different combination of systems, operating conditions and maintenance needs, requiring an investigation tailored to that site.

Alares' healthcare retrocommissioning services focused on how HVAC systems performed in use. The work connected observations and test results to specific recommendations the VA could evaluate.

Alares' retrocommissioning scope

  • Site-specific planning. Developed a retrocommissioning plan for each of the 11 campuses.
  • Field investigation. Inspected HVAC systems and compiled equipment inventories.
  • Performance testing. Functionally tested equipment, including terminal units, and gathered operating data.
  • Analysis. Compared observed system behavior with intended operation to identify improvement opportunities.
  • Recommendations. Documented proposed measures with implementation cost estimates and projected energy savings.

From field evidence to recommendations

The team combined field inspections, functional tests and data analysis to distinguish operating issues from equipment problems. Recommendations were developed for each campus, rather than treating the network as a single building.

The work followed FEMP/DOE and ASHRAE guidance. Its deliverables gave the VA documented measures and estimates for evaluating improvements across the portfolio.

Project deliverables

  • Individual campus retrocommissioning plans
  • Equipment inventories and HVAC performance findings
  • Site-specific improvement recommendations
  • Cost estimates and projected energy savings for proposed measures

The energy savings associated with the recommendations were projections, not measured post-implementation results. The case demonstrates the investigation and documentation needed to assess opportunities before deciding which measures to implement.
